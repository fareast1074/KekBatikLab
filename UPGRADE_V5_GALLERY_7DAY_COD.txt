KekBatikLab Upgrade V5 — 7-Day Ordering, Place-Based COD, and Customer Gallery

What's new
1. Customers can browse the Gallery tab, see each active product picture and price, and tap Order Now to jump into the order form with that product selected.
2. Admins can open Gallery and upload a photo for each product. The image is resized/compressed in the browser and saved on the product record in Firebase Realtime Database, so it also appears in the order product selector.
3. The order calendar displays the next seven consecutive dates and no longer excludes Saturdays or Sundays. Existing admin product schedules and closed-date settings are still enforced.
4. Admin Settings now has Delivery Places & COD Settings. Admins can add/edit/delete places and configure a minimum number of boxes, optional minimum product subtotal (RM), and a COD fee calculation (flat RM fee or percentage of product subtotal).
5. Customers choose a configured place in the order form. The live breakdown shows product subtotal, COD charge, and final total. Orders below the selected place's minimum are blocked with a clear message.
6. Saved orders retain the selected place and the product subtotal/COD fee breakdown. Sales analytics continue to count the product subtotal, not the COD charge.

Setup after deploying
- Sign in as Admin and open Settings → Delivery Places & COD Settings.
- Add the place/area names you serve and configure the minimum order and COD fee for each one.
- Open Gallery as Admin, select each product, upload its photo, and save it.
- Customers can then use Gallery → Order Now or go directly to Order and choose a place.

Firebase paths added/used
- Product images: kekBatikLabApp/products/{productId}/imageUrl
- Delivery/COD places: kekBatikLabApp/settings/checkout_config/places/{placeId}

Note
The app's existing Firebase configuration and authentication flow are preserved. The updated UI was syntax-checked, but live saving/loading should be verified on the deployed app with its Firebase Realtime Database rules and Anonymous Authentication enabled.
